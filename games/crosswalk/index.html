How to play
  • Red runners cross from top to bottom, Blue from left to right. Every lane crosses every other lane.
  • On your turn, roll the die and move one of your runners exactly that many squares.
  • The die shows 1, 2, 3, 1, 2 or ★. On a ★ you choose 1, 2 or 3.
  • Land on an opponent's runner to send it back to its start, and you roll again.
  • Moving past the last square gets your runner home. First to get 4 runners home wins.
